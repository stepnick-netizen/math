Why maths matters right now

During Russian air attacks, someone is solving a problem at that very minute: where a missile or drone is coming from, where it is heading and how to intercept it. The faster and more precise that calculation, the sooner the all-clear comes and the fewer hours children spend in shelters.

Air defence, radar, drones, secure messaging, maps for deminers: it’s all maths. Every problem here trains that kind of thinking. Maybe the next solution that keeps you and your friends safe will be yours.

Why “The Maths Apiary”?

Bees are born mathematicians. Every cell in a honeycomb is a hexagon. Why not a square or a triangle? Take cells with the same area, 1 cm² each, and work out how much wall each one needs. Bees build the walls out of wax, and wax is costly: to make it, bees have to eat a lot of honey.

The hexagon has the shortest walls, so it uses the least wax. Circles on their own are even shorter, but they leave gaps between them. People noticed this bee trick as far back as ancient Rome, but the mathematician Thomas Hales only proved it rigorously in 1999.

An apiary is Ukraine. In 1814 the Ukrainian Petro Prokopovych built the world’s first frame beehive: frames full of honey can be taken out without wrecking the bees’ home. Beekeepers still use hives like this today.

In honour of a teacher. This site is built on the presentations and lessons of maths teacher Ihor Petrovych Pasika (Lyceum No. 157, Kyiv). We take the explanations, examples and problems from his lessons and turn them into games and cheat sheets. That is why the site is named after him: Pasika means “apiary” in Ukrainian.

How to use it

  1. Open a topic on a phone or computer. No sign-up needed.
  2. Stars and progress are saved in the browser on this device. On another device the game starts from the beginning.
  3. In Monomials, Powers, Adjacent angles and Identities, tap "Choose a world and name": pick the meadow, space or bricks, add your name and rename the heroes.
  4. In the "My report" tab, tap "Send my report" and choose WhatsApp, Viber or email. Or copy the report and paste it into a message to your teacher or parents.
