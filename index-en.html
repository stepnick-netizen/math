How to use it

  1. Open a topic on a phone or computer. No sign-up needed.
  2. Stars and progress are saved in the browser on this device. On another device the game starts from the beginning.
  3. In Monomials, tap "Choose a world and name": pick the meadow, space or bricks, add your name and rename the heroes.
  4. In the "My report" tab, tap "Send my report" and choose WhatsApp, Viber or email. Or copy the report and paste it into a message to your teacher or parents.
