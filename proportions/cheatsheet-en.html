Proportions on one page

Hi! Bulya and I drew everything important about proportions. Follow the arrows!

1

Ratio

Divide one number by the other. We read it as "one to two".

2

Proportion

Two ratios that equal the same number. We put "=" between them.

3

Outer and inner numbers

The outer numbers sit at the ends, the inner ones in the middle.

The outer ones are like me and Ponya at each end of the sofa, and the inner ones squeeze in between!

4

The big rule: cross-multiply

Write it as fractions and multiply across. Outer numbers multiplied = inner numbers multiplied. Equal? Then it's a proportion!

5

Finding the missing number

  1. Multiply the pair that doesn't have x (both outer or both inner).
  2. Divide by x's partner in its pair.
6

Swapping to make new proportions

Swap places and it's still a proportion, because the products stay the same.

  • ① swap the outer numbers: 24 : 40 = 6 : 10
  • ② swap the inner numbers: 10 : 6 = 40 : 24
  • ①+② swap both pairs: 24 : 6 = 40 : 10
7

Proportion in real life

2 kg of hay lasts 5 days. How much hay for 15 days? 3 times as many days, so 3 times as much hay.

2 : 5 = x : 15 → x = (2 × 15) ÷ 5 = 6 kg

Found the answer? Put it back in and cross-multiply. If it matches, well done!
