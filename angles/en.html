1

A straight angle

Rays OA and OB are opposite rays: together they make a straight line. The angle between them is 180°.

That’s half a full turn (360°).

2

Adjacent angles

Two angles are adjacent on a straight line if they share one side and their other sides are opposite rays.

∠COA and ∠AOB are adjacent: together they make the straight angle COB.

Adjacent angles are two pieces of one straight angle.

3

Key rule: adjacent angles add up to 180°

∠NAK + ∠KAM = 180°. However you tilt ray AK, the two adjacent angles always make a straight angle together.

One angle grows, its neighbour shrinks. Together they always make 180°!

4

Find the partner

Know one angle? Subtract it from 180°: partner = 180° − known angle.

Check: 72° + 108° = 180°.

5

The angle bisector

The bisector is the ray that splits an angle in half. Angle = 2 × half.

Split it fairly, like a treat for two!

6

Three consequences

The partner of a right angle is right. The partner of an acute angle is obtuse. The partner of an obtuse angle is acute.

Also: if two angles are equal, their partners are equal too. Two angles that are both partners of the same angle are equal.

7

Angles in the ratio 4 : 5

Two adjacent angles are in the ratio 4 : 5. Split 180° into parts.

Check: 80° + 100° = 180°, and 80 : 100 = 4 : 5.

8

One is 30° bigger

Call the smaller angle x and the bigger one x + 30°. Together they make 180°.

Answer: 75° and 105°. Check: 105° − 75° = 30°.

9

Angles in real life

A laptop lid is opened to 110° from the keyboard. So it makes 70° with the rest of the table line.

Builders, navigators and game designers work out angles like these every day.
