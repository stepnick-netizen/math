What is a percentage?

Per cent means "out of a hundred". Picture a blanket with 100 stitches. One stitch is 1%, ten stitches are 10%, and all hundred are 100%, the whole blanket.

Three key skills

Find a percentage of an amount

Divide by 100 to find 1%, then multiply by the percentage.

30% of 80 = 80 ÷ 100 × 30 = 24

Find the whole from a percentage

Divide by the percentage to find 1%, then multiply by 100.

9 is 30% of a number.
9 ÷ 30 × 100 = 30

Write an amount as a percentage

Divide the part by the whole, then multiply by 100.

15 out of 20 = 15 ÷ 20 × 100 = 75%

Quick tricks

50% is a half, divide by 2 25% is a quarter, divide by 4 20%, divide by 5 10%, divide by 10 1%, divide by 100 30% off → you pay 70%

Ponya says: the % sign is two little zeros with a line between them, just like the 100 in 1/100. Clever, isn't it?
